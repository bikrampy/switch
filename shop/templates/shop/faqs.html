Frequently Asked Questions

Here are the answers to some of the most common questions our customers ask. If you still have queries, feel free to submit a message using the form.

How to order products?
Browse our products, add the items you want to your cart, and proceed to checkout to place your order.

Is online purchase compulsory?
No, you can also visit our local stores to browse and make a purchase in person if you prefer.

Can I get discounts on products?
We occasionally offer promotional discounts. Keep an eye on our homepage or sign up for our newsletter for updates.

What information is needed to order?
Please provide your full name, shipping destination address, phone number, and email to complete an order.

Can I cancel my order?
Orders can be cancelled before dispatch (normally within 24 hours of placement) by contacting our customer service.

What's your return policy?
We offer free global returns within 30 days of receiving your item. Items must be in original condition with tags intact.
